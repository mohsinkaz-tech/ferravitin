ABOUT SHAJARA

A purpose rooted
in community.

Shajara Capital Inc. is a British Columbia company focused on developing Islamic finance pathways for Muslim families and institutions in North America.

Why we are here.

Our aim is to create space for conversations about faith, financial needs and the wellbeing of our communities.

We are beginning in British Columbia, with a community-first approach that values careful listening and clear communication. Families, mosques, institutions and prospective partners are welcome to help shape that conversation.

Our public launch brings that purpose into action through a charitable appeal supporting Shaukat Khanum.
