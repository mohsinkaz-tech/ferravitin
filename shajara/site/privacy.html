This notice explains the information requested through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address and inquiry type. Organization and message are optional. We also record the submission time, a lead identifier, the page source and your consent choices. Please avoid submitting sensitive financial, identity or health information.

Why we collect it

Shajara Capital Inc. uses your details to respond to your inquiry and manage the conversation. Occasional updates require a separate, optional consent choice. A charitable donation is made on JustGiving and is subject to that platform’s privacy notice.

Where it is handled

The website is hosted using AWS Amplify. Website requests may be processed through AWS’s global delivery network. Inquiry records are intended for a private Google Sheets register, accessible to authorized people handling Shajara inquiries. Google may process information outside Canada. We do not promise exclusively Canadian data residency.

Retention and access

We review inquiry records after 12 months and keep them only where needed for an ongoing inquiry, consented communications or applicable obligations. You can request access, correction or withdrawal of consent through our contact form. We do not sell inquiry details.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not add advertising pixels, analytics cookies or third-party marketing trackers. Normal hosting and delivery logs may record technical request information for operation and security. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.
