# Shajara Capital website — V1

Prepared October 14, 2026. Based on the owner’s handoff, brand guide and approved launch scope.

## Direction and scope

Two directions considered: **Institutional trust** uses restrained typography, formal navigation and clear disclosures; **Community warmth** centers the organizer’s story and invitation. Recommendation implemented: institutional trust across the main website, with community warmth on the fundraiser page. Primary CTA: collaboration inquiry. Community CTA: watch the invitation and donate on JustGiving. No investor solicitation.

English site; Arabic opening retained on the community appeal.

## Design and accessibility

Actual Shajara logo and Switzer fonts. Navy #041E4C, blue #1464C7 and gold #F9C13D. Gold is reserved for accents and text on navy; it is unsuitable for ordinary text on white because contrast is below WCAG AA. Body copy uses navy/muted dark text on white. The design includes semantic headings, keyboard focus states, skip navigation, responsive layout, form labels, native video controls with burned-in captions, reduced-motion support and separate consent controls. Automated and visual checks do not constitute a full accessibility certification.

## Privacy and deployment

AWS app in Canada (ca-central-1), with global delivery; Google Sheets processing may occur outside Canada. No advertising trackers or analytics cookies added. A consent banner is unnecessary for the current cookie-free application, but reassess before adding analytics or pixels. Data collection is limited to inquiry information, not financial or health details. Privacy wording is a draft for owner review, not a claim of completed legal compliance.

Lead spreadsheet created privately. Google web-app authorization is a remaining owner-controlled step; the form stays disabled until the bridge is live and a test row is verified. Primary domain: shajara.capital. shajara.ca serves the same site with canonical metadata pointing to shajara.capital. Existing Framer DNS must be captured and changed only after the AWS site and certificate are verified. Existing mail records must remain unchanged.
