A NOTE FROM MY HEART

Let’s make room
for someone else’s hope.

For a family facing cancer, the journey can feel overwhelming. I hope we can come together with sincerity, care and a shared sense of responsibility.

My invitation is simple: ten founding supporters, each considering a gift of CAD $500 to $1,000. These are our aspirations, not funds already raised. Every expression of interest begins a conversation.

Please register below if you would like to hear more. Donations are made through my JustGiving page, the official fundraising tool for this appeal. If you cannot give at this level, your du‘a and a thoughtful introduction are also welcome.

May Allah accept our intentions, grant shifa to those who are unwell, and place barakah in what we give. Ameen.

With gratitude and du‘a,
Mohsin Kazi
