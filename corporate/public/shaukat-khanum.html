Is this campaign authorized by CERF or Shaukat Khanum?

This is an independent invitation hosted by Mohsin Kazi through BioScience Global. Campaign-specific authorization from CERF or Shaukat Khanum has not been verified. No official endorsement, partnership, event sponsorship benefit, or logo permission is claimed.

CERF publishes a fundraiser setup route. Official campaign arrangements and attribution can be confirmed with office@cerfcanada.com.

Will I get a tax receipt for registering or pledging?

No. Interest registration and pledges do not generate tax receipts. A receipt for an eligible actual donation is handled by the receiving charity. Ask CERF about eligibility, payment options, and any sponsorship benefits before giving.

Does my registration reserve one of the 10 places?

The goal is a circle of 10 partners. Registration expresses interest; Mohsin will confirm participation individually. There is no public live count and no claim that any funds have already been raised.

How are my contact details used?

BioScience Global collects your name, email, optional organization and phone, intended amount, preferred contact method, and note solely to coordinate this campaign enquiry. Details are stored in its restricted Google Sheets lead records with a reference and consent record; its team is notified of the enquiry.

Your registration does not subscribe you to marketing, publish your name, or send your details to CERF. To correct or request removal of your details, contact mohsin.kazi@biosciglobal.net with your reference. Charity websites have their own privacy policies. See the BioScience Global privacy notice.
