Questions pharmacists ask

Who sells and ships the product?+

Bioclean Innovation Inc. DBA Bioscience Global is the seller of record. We own the inventory, issue the invoice, take payment and ship to your pharmacy. Nadeem Zia supports your account; he does not buy or resell stock.

How does payment work?+

All orders are prepaid. Your opening order is paid when you sign the purchase order. Each monthly shipment is charged before it ships, by pre-authorized debit, card or EFT.

What if Ferravitin doesn't sell in my store?+

Within 4 months of your first delivery, you can return unopened, saleable bottles once, for the full price you paid. The bottles need at least 6 months left before expiry. We collect the stock and refund you within 30 days.

Can I set my own retail price?+

Yes. The suggested retail prices are $59.99 for Daily Maintenance and $69.99 for Pregnancy Formula. Ferravitin.com sells at suggested retail, so we don't undercut participating pharmacies.

Are there allergen considerations?+

Yes. The peptide carrier is egg-derived, and both products are labelled as containing egg. Counsel customers with an egg allergy accordingly.

What materials can my team use?+

Use only Bioscience Global-supplied materials. They stay within the claims authorized under each product licence. Please don't make comparative or absorption-percentage claims.
